# قائف

## Purpose
An Arabic operational workspace transitioning to reusable Seraj-monitored LPG-cylinder transport cages between GASCO filling facilities and retailers. The October 8 map revision covers seven filling stations, illustrative service zones and retailers, and animated shipment routes. Stable display cylinder identities now have membership intervals, journey evidence and illustrative arrival inspections. An operational persisted registry and field integrations remain future work. Earlier pipe-saddle, drone and inspection workflows remain recoverable under separate legacy routes. The main map, saddle, cylinder and inspection entries now concern LPG shipment display workflows.

## Platform
web

## Users and scene
An asset engineer reviews this dashboard on a laptop in a brightly lit control room or hackathon venue; a drone operator or inspector uses a smaller screen to upload evidence and update a task. Clear Arabic labels, visible provenance and reliable controls matter more than decoration.

## Evidence boundaries
Simulator readings are research output, not calibrated field readings. The pipe simulator includes H2S; the shipment dashboard explicitly represents that numeric channel as an LPG display proxy. Inventory locations/routes are schematic demonstration data. Uploaded camera images retain their actual provenance. No forced alarm or prewritten result may appear as a computed observation. Image analysis can describe visible evidence; it cannot prove defect depth or internal cracks. Gemini is optional and secrets remain server-side. Source-clock, simulation elapsed time and camera capture time remain distinct.
